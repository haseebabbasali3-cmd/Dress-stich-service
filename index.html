Frequently asked questions

How do I order a design?

Choose a design and use its WhatsApp button, or fill in the order form. Confirm final price, availability and delivery/collection details directly with an owner.

Can I add my own dress photos and prices?

Yes. Open Owner Admin from the footer, sign in to the local demo panel, and use Manage Products to add, edit or remove listings. This starter version stores changes in this browser only.

Are the prices fixed?

Prices displayed on a product are entered by the shop. Please confirm the final price and any stitching charges with the owner before ordering.
